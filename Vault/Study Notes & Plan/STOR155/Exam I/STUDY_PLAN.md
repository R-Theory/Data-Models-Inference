## 💡 STUDY STRATEGIES FOR MASTERY

### Active Recall (Most Effective!)
- Don't just read - TEST yourself
- Cover up answers, try to solve
- Explain concepts out loud
- Teach someone else (even an imaginary student)

### Spaced Repetition
- Don't cram everything in one day
- Review material multiple times
- First time: deep learning
- Second time: reinforcement
- Third time: quick recall

### Practice with Real Data
Use the datasets provided:

Try these activities:

### Handwritten Practice
- Write formulas by hand 10 times each
- Solve problems on paper (not just mentally)
- Create your own formula sheet

### Form Study Group
- Quiz each other
- Explain concepts to peers
- Different perspectives help
- Stay focused on content!
