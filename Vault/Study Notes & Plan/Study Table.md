### **Machine Learning Takeaway:**

This entire course is essentially "**Data Science Foundations**" - every topic directly transfers to ML workflows. You're learning data literacy before model building, which is why ~80% of a data scientist's time is spent on data cleaning/exploration (Topics 1-5) and only ~20% on modeling (Topics 6-7). Master these fundamentals and you'll understand why garbage data → garbage models!
